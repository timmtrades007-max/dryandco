FAQ

Straight answers for customers — and for whoever’s running the chats.

How do I order?

Add to bag on the site, then tap Order via WhatsApp. We confirm size, suburb and total, then send payment details.

Do you ship nationwide?

Yes. Free shipping from R1 200. Under that, we quote shipping in chat before you pay.

Dogs and cats?

Yes — full Dry Ritual and Leather Walk for dogs, plus a separate Cat Estate collection (caves, towers, litter cabinet, harnesses and more).

How long does delivery take?

We usually dispatch in 2–4 business days from the Western Cape.

Can I exchange a size?

Yes within 30 days if unused and clean. Engraved name discs are final sale unless faulty.

Do you do wholesale?

Yes for boutiques and markets. Message us on the Contact page with “Wholesale”.
