How to run dry&co day to day

A simple ops rhythm so the brand can live without mum doing every chat.

01

Morning (15 min)

Check WhatsApp orders. Confirm sizes. Send payment link. Update a shared Google Sheet: order · suburb · items · paid · shipped.

02

Pack & ship

Print packing slip from the chat. Include care card. Photo the sealed parcel. Book courier. Paste tracking back into WhatsApp.

03

Content (3× week)

Post one product + one lifestyle frame + one story beat (Milo / cats / Cape rain). Link Pulse page on launch weeks.

04

Bestsellers to push first

Dogs: Dry Robe, Paw Butter, Car Hammock, Check Blanket.
Cats: Cave, Fountain, Scratcher, Catnip Trio, Litter Cabinet.

05

Sets = easier selling

Offer Dry Ritual Set, Cat Estate Set, Happy Cat Play Set. One message, higher basket, happier customer.

06

Money

Free ship from R1 200. Quote under that before payment. Keep 30-day exchange promise. Engraved discs = final sale.
