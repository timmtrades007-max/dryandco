Fit

Size guide

Measure once. Order with confidence. If fit isn’t right, we’ll sort an exchange.

Step 1 Soft tape around the widest part of the chest (behind front legs).
Step 2 Neck: where a collar sits comfortably — two fingers of space.
Step 3 Between sizes? Size up for coats and robes; size to neck for collars.
